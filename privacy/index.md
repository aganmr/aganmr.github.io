## Short version

Our apps store all of your data **on your phone only**. We do not run a
server, we do not create accounts, and we do not collect, upload, or share
your data. Deleting an app deletes its data.

## Data we store

Each app stores the information you enter, on your device:

- **Fuel & Charge Tracker:** vehicle details (name, type, odometer); fuel,
  charge and expense logs (odometer, volume, cost, date); maintenance items
  and schedule; app settings (unit system, currency, default prices, plan
  status)
- **Spoolish:** print records (name, printer, filaments, time, computed
  cost); filament spools; printer profiles; app settings (region,
  electricity rate, machine wear)

This data is stored in a local database on your phone. It is never transmitted
to us or any third party. In live mode, Spoolish also reads print progress
from your own printer over your local network — between your devices only,
never to us.

## Data we do not collect

- No account, email address, or personal identity
- No location data
- No analytics or usage tracking
- No crash reporting

## Third-party services

### Google AdMob (where "Ads" is yes)

The free tier displays ads (banners and rewarded videos) provided by Google
AdMob. Google may collect and use device and ad-related information (such as
the Android ID) for ad personalisation as described in [Google's Ads
policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads). Purchasing Pro or Remove Ads disables all advertising.

### RevenueCat (where "In-app purchases" is yes)

The apps offer one-time purchases (for Fuel & Charge Tracker: Pro
Lifetime and Remove Ads) processed through RevenueCat and your
device's app store (Google Play). RevenueCat and Google process the
payment information needed to complete your purchase. We do not
receive your payment details. Because these are one-time purchases
(there are no subscriptions), there is nothing to cancel; refunds
are handled by Google Play under its refund policy. See [RevenueCat's
privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) for details.

## Children's privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect
any data from children. No data of any kind is collected by the apps
themselves.

## Your rights

Because all data lives on your device:

- **Access:** your data is visible in the app at all times.
- **Export:** where an app offers it, you can export your data at any time
  (Fuel & Charge Tracker: PDF or CSV - a Pro feature, and available once
  per week to free users who watch a short video).
- **Deletion:** delete data in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all
  of it.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our apps evolve (for example, if we add cloud
features in a future version). If we add any data collection, we will update

with a new "Last updated" date.
